# Background reading checklist

Official verifiers source/API, dependency compatibility, Prime environment concepts,
Harvey LAB README/architecture, free model access and cloud hosting limitations
were consulted during AI-assisted planning. No claim is made that the owner has
personally completed the PDF's 45-minute preparatory reading.

Owner checklist: inspect 10–15 real environments at
https://app.primeintellect.ai/dashboard/environments ; record names and one reward
strength/weakness each. Read https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/verifiers at pinned
v0.1.14, https://docs.primeintellect.ai/verifiers/environments ,
https://github.com/harveyai/harvey-labs and its docs/architecture.md. Contrast open
prose judging with deterministic verifiers and low correctness caps.

Current online verifiers docs also describe a newer API; this assignment adapter
deliberately targets the pinned legacy Environment/SingleTurnEnv/ToolEnv interface.
